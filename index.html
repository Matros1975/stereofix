Mono and balance for each audio output.

StereoFix gives every sound output on your Mac its own mono and balance setting, and switches them automatically when you change output.

The problem

macOS has one mono switch and one balance slider for everything. If a MacBook speaker crackles, or you hear in one ear, the fix you need for one output is wrong for the next: mono on the speakers also makes your headphones mono, and every time you switch you go back into System Settings.

How it works

  1. Click the laptop icon in the menu bar.
  2. For a device, switch on Use profile and choose Mono and Balance.
  3. Whenever your Mac switches to that output, StereoFix applies its profile. Devices without a profile keep macOS's normal behaviour.

Made for

  • A broken or crackling left speaker. Built-in speakers: Mono on, Balance fully right. You hear both stereo channels from the working speaker, while headphones stay in stereo.
  • Hearing in one ear. Mono just for your headphones or AirPods; other outputs stay in stereo.
  • Balance that keeps drifting. Each device keeps the balance you set.

Asks for nothing

No microphone, Accessibility or Screen Recording permission. StereoFix runs in the App Sandbox, has no account, makes no network connections and collects no data. It never processes or records audio: it only switches macOS's own mono and balance settings.

Free

StereoFix is free. If it helps you, an optional tip in the app keeps it going. Nothing unlocks; there is no subscription.
