Mono audio for headphones or AirPods only on Mac

Short answer: macOS has one switch, System Settings › Accessibility › Audio › Play stereo audio as mono. It makes every output mono at once: headphones, AirPods, the built-in speakers and external speakers. To have mono on your headphones only, you have to switch it on and off by hand whenever you change output.

StereoFix, a free Mac app, gives each output its own mono setting and applies it automatically, so your AirPods are mono and your speakers stay stereo.

Who needs mono audio

  • Hearing in one ear (single-sided deafness, unilateral hearing loss): in stereo, sounds recorded only on the other channel never reach you. Mono sends everything to both ears.
  • One earbud at a time: with one AirPod in, mono makes sure you hear the whole recording.
  • A broken earbud or headphone side.

Turn on mono in macOS (all outputs)

  1. Open System Settings from the Apple menu.
  2. Click Accessibility, then Audio.
  3. Turn on Play stereo audio as mono.

This is a single system-wide setting. Apple has per-device options for AirPods and Beats, but not a per-device mono switch on the Mac.

Mono only on headphones or AirPods, automatically

  1. Install StereoFix from the Mac App Store (free) and click the laptop icon in the menu bar.
  2. Turn off “Play stereo audio as mono” in Accessibility › Audio, so StereoFix can decide per output.
  3. Under your headphones or AirPods, switch on Use profile and Mono. Leave the speakers without a profile.

When you connect those headphones, StereoFix turns mono on; when you switch back to the speakers, it turns mono off. Each device is remembered, including ones that aren't connected right now. Where a device has a balance control, you can also set its balance.

StereoFix doesn't record or process audio and needs no Accessibility, microphone or screen permission. It collects no data.

Frequently asked questions

How do I make only my headphones mono on a Mac?
macOS can't do that by itself: its mono switch applies to every output. StereoFix, a free Mac app, gives each output its own mono setting and switches it automatically when you change output.
Where is the mono audio setting on Mac?
System Settings, Accessibility, Audio, Play stereo audio as mono. In older macOS versions it is in System Preferences, Accessibility, Audio.
Does mono work with AirPods on a Mac?
Yes. With mono on, both AirPods play the same mixed sound. Spatial Audio and head tracking are not available while mono is on.
Is mono audio worse quality?
Mono loses the left-right placement of sounds, not their detail. If you hear in one ear, you couldn't use that placement anyway, and mono makes sure nothing is missing.
Does mono apply to all apps?
Yes. Mono is one macOS setting for the current output, so it applies to every app playing through it.
