Privacy Policy

MonoProfiles collects no data. Last updated 16 October 2026.

What the app does

MonoProfiles changes two macOS sound settings — system mono and an output device's balance — according to the profiles you create. It does not process, record or listen to any audio and does not use the microphone.

Data collected

None. MonoProfiles has no analytics, no tracking, no advertising, no accounts and no network access of its own. Nothing about you or your devices is sent to us or to anyone else.

Data stored on your Mac

Your profiles (device names and identifiers, mono and balance values) and a few app settings are stored only on your Mac, inside the app's sandbox container. Deleting the app removes them.

Tips

Optional tips are processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. We receive no payment details or personal information from Apple about who tipped. Apple's privacy policy applies to those purchases.

Children

MonoProfiles collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be published on this page with a new date.
